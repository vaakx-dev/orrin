# <img src="assets/logo.svg" width="48" height="48" alt="" align="center"> Orrin

Orrin is one program on each of your PCs that owns your projects: their files, worker views, landing, builds and sync. AI agents and people work side by side in cheap views, Orrin lands their changes on main, builds every step once and shares the results, and keeps your PCs in step. Git is an optional backup.

## Install

Download the binary for your platform from [Releases](../../releases) and put it on your `PATH`.

## Use

```sh
orrin serve
```
